About

Hello!

My name is Chen-Yu (Irvin) Lee, and I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Political Science at Pennsylvania State University, pursuing a dual title in Social Data Analytics. My research examines state repression, authoritarian politics, and historical political economy, drawing on causal inference, geospatial analysis, and network analysis to study the dynamics and consequences of state repression in autocratic regimes. Before Penn State, I completed an MSc in Political Science (with Distinction) at the University of Essex and an MA and BA in Political Science at National Taiwan University.

When I'm not studying political science, I enjoy hiking, cycling, playing basketball, and backpacking.
