About

Overview

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Political Science at Pennsylvania State University, pursuing a dual title in Social Data Analytics. My research examines state repression, authoritarian politics, and historical political economy, drawing on causal inference, geospatial analysis, and network analysis to study how coercive institutions form, persist, and leave lasting imprints on political and economic life.

Before Penn State, I completed an MSc in Political Science (with Distinction) at the University of Essex and an MA and BA in Political Science at National Taiwan University.
